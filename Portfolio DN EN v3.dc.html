05

Method and repertoire

A method of my own — and the repertoire to choose from.

Two different things: the method is how I lead, always; the repertoire is the set of tools I draw on to adapt it to your case.

The method

No process is waived by omission — it is waived by a recorded decision.

The rule underpins the whole document: every simplification is written down, with reasoning and approval — never by oversight.

The method is written down in a playbook of my own: a six-phase life cycle, each phase closed by a met exit criterion — never by calendar pressure — and triangulated against four international standards, so governance never rests on my opinion alone.

My commitment to you

You will not discover a problem too late.

If something is drifting off course, you will know early — with the context and the action plan alongside. Not through a problem that has already blown up.

And in the end, you will not just have a system delivered. You will have the evidence that it produced the return that justified the investment.

A serious problem turns the project red, and it stays red until it is genuinely resolved — I do not dress it up to look better.
